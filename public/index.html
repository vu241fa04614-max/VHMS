Platform Capabilities

Everything You Need to
Manage a Modern Hostel

A comprehensive suite of management tools designed for the complex operational needs of a university residential facility.

Student Management

Comprehensive student profiles with registration details, department info, emergency contacts, and hostel allocation history. Full CRUD with search and filtering.

Room Allocation System

Intelligent room allocation with capacity enforcement, duplicate prevention, and block-wise visual occupancy tracking. Real-time availability monitoring.

Outpass Management

Digital leave application with multi-type outpass support (day/night/weekend/vacation). Full approval workflow with warden review and status tracking.

Complaint Tracking

Categorized complaint submission (electrical, plumbing, WiFi, security, etc.) with priority levels, assignment workflows, and resolution tracking.

Fee Management

Detailed fee records with hostel, mess, maintenance components. Payment tracking, receipt generation, overdue detection, and financial analytics.

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time occupancy analytics, fee collection reports, complaint statistics, and hostel-wise student distribution with Chart.js visualizations.

Mess Menu System

Weekly mess menu management with breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner schedules. Students can view the current week's menu and provide food feedback.

Announcements & Alerts

Role-targeted announcement publishing with pinning, urgency levels, and in-app notification delivery to students, wardens, and staff.

Secure Authentication

JWT-based multi-role authentication with bcrypt password hashing, session management, role-based route protection, and activity logging.

How It Works

Simple. Fast. Transparent.

From admission to room allocation to daily hostel operations — everything managed through one integrated platform.

01

Student Registration

Admin registers students with complete profiles including department, year, emergency contacts, and academic details.

02

Room Allocation

Intelligent room assignment based on availability, capacity constraints, and hostel type. No duplicates, no over-allocation.

03

Day-to-Day Operations
